# Privacy Policy

Version: 2026-05-21

This privacy policy explains how Lendly collects, uses, and protects personal data. We only collect the minimum personal data necessary to operate the marketplace and support bookings.

We use personal data for:
- account registration and authentication
- communication about bookings
- legal compliance and dispute resolution
- service improvements

We do not share personal data with third parties, except as required by law or to deliver the service.

If you request deletion of your account, we will retain the minimum necessary records to preserve booking and transaction history.
