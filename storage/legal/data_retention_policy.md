# Data Retention Policy

Version: 2026-05-21

Lendly retains personal data only as long as necessary to support active accounts, bookings, and legal obligations.

- Session data is kept for active refresh sessions.
- Revoked sessions are removed after 30 days.
- Expired sessions are removed after the refresh token retention period.
- User deletion requests are recorded and personal fields may be anonymized to preserve booking integrity.
