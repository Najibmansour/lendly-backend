# Terms OF Service for Lendly
## Marketplace Terms of Service (ToS) agreements typically serve to define the rules for buyers and sellers, limit platform liability, and establish fee structures. Online marketplaces (e.g., Amazon, Etsy) generally include clauses on user conduct, intellectual property rights, payment and refund policies, and dispute resolution.

#### Key components found in marketplace ToS samples include:

Acceptance of Terms: Users agree to the terms by clicking "I Agree" (clickwrap) or by using the site (browsewrap). 
Seller Obligations: Sellers must provide accurate product descriptions, comply with listing requirements, and handle customer service or returns as defined by the platform.
Platform Liability: Marketplaces often disclaim warranties, stating they provide the platform "as is" and are not responsible for third-party transactions or product defects. 
Fees and Payments: Clear terms regarding transaction fees, payment processing, and when funds are released to sellers.
Intellectual Property: Policies protecting the marketplace’s brand and providing mechanisms for reporting copyright or trademark infringement. 
Specific examples include Google Workspace Marketplace, which governs developers and users through its Terms of Service and Program Policies, and AWS Marketplace, which integrates its terms into Section 20 of its Service Terms. GitHub Marketplace ToS clarify that the platform is a third-party beneficiary to agreements between users and product providers, limiting direct liability for developer products.
